About 70 Chale

Celebrating 70 Years of Freedom, Unity and Progress

Welcome to 70 Chale, a platform created to celebrate Ghana’s 70th Independence Anniversary. As Ghana approaches this historic milestone on 6th March 2027, we honour the courage, sacrifices and determination of the people who fought for our nation's freedom.

Who We Are

The name 70 Chale reflects the Ghanaian spirit of friendship, togetherness and national pride. It represents our shared identity, our rich cultural heritage and our hopes for a brighter future.

This platform brings Ghanaians together to celebrate a historic chapter in our nation’s story and to honour the people who made freedom possible.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring Ghanaians together to celebrate 70 years of independence by promoting patriotism, preserving our cultural heritage and sharing the story of Ghana's journey from independence to the present day.

Through this website, we aim to create a space where citizens, especially the youth, can learn about our history, appreciate our achievements and become inspired to contribute to the nation's development.

What We Seek to Achieve

1. Celebrate Ghana's History

Highlight the historical events, national heroes and sacrifices that led to Ghana gaining independence on 6th March 1957.

2. Promote Patriotism and Unity

Encourage Ghanaians at home and abroad to embrace the national colours, respect our diversity and work together toward a united nation.

3. Educate and Inspire the Youth

Provide information about Ghana's independence journey, cultural heritage, national symbols and achievements to help young people understand their role in shaping the future.

4. Showcase Ghanaian Culture

Celebrate our traditions, music, food, fashion, languages, creativity and the cultural diversity that makes Ghana unique.

5. Encourage Participation

Provide a platform for people to engage with the anniversary celebration, share ideas, participate in activities and express what being Ghanaian means to them.

6. Inspire the Next Generation

Use the 70th anniversary as an opportunity to reflect on our progress, recognise our challenges and encourage innovation, responsible citizenship and national development.

Our Vision

We envision a Ghana where every citizen takes pride in our heritage, values our independence and actively contributes to building a peaceful, prosperous and united country.

Our Message to Every Ghanaian

Seventy years of independence is more than a milestone. It is a reminder of where we started, how far we have come and the future we can build together.

Red for the blood of our freedom fighters. Gold for our nation's wealth. Green for our rich land. Black for the star of African freedom.

One Nation. One People. One Future.
