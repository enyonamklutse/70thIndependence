Travel essentials

Helpful planning notes

☀️

Best time to visit

November to March brings dry, sunny weather and ideal conditions for city tours and coastal escapes.

🚐

Getting around

Domestic flights, intercity buses, and ride-hailing apps make longer routes comfortable and flexible.

🍲

Food to try

Fufu, waakye, jollof, kelewele, grilled tilapia, fresh palm nut soup, and local fruit juices.

🎒

What to pack

Light fabrics, sun protection, comfortable shoes, a light rain layer, and a reusable water bottle.
